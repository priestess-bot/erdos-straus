## Least Open Quantifier

For a finite fixed-gap menu \(\mathcal M\), define

\[
x_m=\frac{p+m}{4},
\]

\[
\mathcal D_I(p,m)=\{d:d\mid x_m^2,\ m\mid px_m+d\},
\]

\[
\mathcal D_{II}(p,m)=\{d:d\mid x_m^2,\ d\le x_m,\ m\mid x_m+d\}.
\]

The exact remaining terminal-prefix predicate is

\[
\mathrm{MISS}_{\mathcal M}(p)
\iff
\forall m\in\mathcal M,
\mathcal D_I(p,m)=\mathcal D_{II}(p,m)=\varnothing.
\]

No current theorem proves that this predicate is empty or infinite on either
C8 ray after the core, \(q=1\ G\), and roughness guards. A finite scan or the
local q1 schedule is not evidence for either alternative.
